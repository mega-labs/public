Privacy Policy

Bobr Runner

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Overview

Bobr Runner ("the App") is developed by PabloSoft. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App. The App can be played without creating an account.

Information We Collect

Advertising Data

The App uses CAS.AI (CleverAdsSolutions) for ad mediation, which may work with multiple advertising networks. These services may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • General location data (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

Game Services Data

Leaderboards, the daily and weekly rankings, cloud saving of your progress and the remote game configuration are provided by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). To make them work the App creates an anonymous player identifier on first launch and may store:

  • The anonymous player identifier and, if you choose one, your player name
  • Your scores, run statistics and rankings
  • Your game progress (coins, unlocks, trophies, settings) for cloud save
  • Device and app version information needed to serve the right configuration

Your player name is shown to other players on the leaderboards. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to be identified. Optionally, you may link your anonymous profile to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) to show achievements there; those services are governed by Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

Analytics and Crash Data

We use Unity Analytics and crash reporting to improve the App. These services collect anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, features used, run lengths), crash reports and performance data, and device information (model, OS version, app version). This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Locally Stored Data

Your profile, settings and best runs are also stored locally on your device. You can erase them at any time from the App's Settings ("Reset save").

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional purchases (remove interstitial ads, coin packs, keys, a starter pack, the season premium track) processed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled by the respective platform's billing system.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with:

  • Advertising partners — through CAS.AI mediation, for the purpose of serving relevant ads
  • Unity Technologies — for leaderboards, cloud save, remote configuration, analytics and crash reporting
  • Google and Apple — for purchases and, if you link them, for Play Games or Game Center achievements

If you purchase "Remove interstitials", interstitial ads are no longer served; rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

Data Retention

Leaderboard scores are kept for the duration of their ranking period and in the hall of fame of past winners. Cloud-saved progress is kept while you use the App. Analytics data is retained according to Unity's default retention policies. Advertising data is managed by the respective ad networks according to their own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device settings.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data, including your player name, scores and cloud save
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below with your player name; we will delete your player data within 30 days. You can also opt out of personalized ads through your device's privacy settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Consent

On first launch, the App presents a consent dialog (powered by CAS.AI's Consent Flow) that complies with GDPR and other applicable regulations. You can manage your ad preferences at any time through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
