Privacy Policy

Bóbr: The Great Dam

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Overview

Bóbr: The Great Dam ("the App") is developed by PabloSoft. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles. The App can be played without creating an account. It contains optional rewarded ads.

Information We Collect

The App has no accounts, no leaderboards, no chat and no in-app purchases. The only feature that uses data is advertising: when a stage goes wrong, the App can offer you a rewarded ad that lets you carry on. Ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one; there are no banners and no ads that interrupt play.

Advertising Data

The App uses CAS.AI (CleverAdsSolutions) for ad mediation, which may work with multiple advertising networks. These services may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • General location data (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions, and the ad SDKs' own crash and performance reports

Locally Stored Data

Your game progress (completed stages, collected logs and coins, unlocked friends) and your settings are stored only on your device. They never leave it. You can erase them at any time from the App's Settings or by uninstalling the App.

App Store Services

The App is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those stores may collect information about downloads, installs and crashes under their own privacy policies; we receive only aggregated, anonymous reports from them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The advertising data above is shared with advertising partners through CAS.AI mediation, for the purpose of serving ads. We do not receive or keep it ourselves.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

We hold no personal data about you ourselves; the advertising data is held by the ad networks. Contact us at the address below and we will pass your request on. You can also opt out of personalized ads through your device's privacy settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Consent

On first launch, the App presents a consent dialog (powered by CAS.AI's Consent Flow) that complies with GDPR and other applicable regulations. You can manage your ad preferences at any time through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
