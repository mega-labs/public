Privacy Policy

Bóbr: The Great Dam

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Overview

Bóbr: The Great Dam ("the App") is developed by PabloSoft. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles. The App can be played without creating an account and without an internet connection.

Information We Collect

The current version of the App does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no leaderboards, no accounts and no chat, and it does not ask for any device permission.

Locally Stored Data

Your game progress (completed stages, collected logs and coins, unlocked friends) and your settings are stored only on your device. They never leave it. You can erase them at any time from the App's Settings or by uninstalling the App.

App Store Services

The App is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those stores may collect information about downloads, installs and crashes under their own privacy policies; we receive only aggregated, anonymous reports from them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data, because the App does not collect any.

Future Versions

If a later version adds features that need data (for example advertising or online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, the changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above, and the store listing's data safety information will be updated to match.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about your data, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
